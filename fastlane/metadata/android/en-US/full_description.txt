A fast, private gallery for your photos and videos, with the editing tools you actually need.

<b>Browse</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Photos</b>: everything grouped by day. Sort by date, name, size or randomly, show only photos or videos, choose 2 to 6 columns.</li>
<li><b>Albums</b>: your folders, sorted by date modified, date created or name.</li>
<li>Full-screen viewer with pinch and double-tap zoom, and a video player.</li>
</ul>

<b>Organize</b>
<ul>
<li>Long-press then drag to select many items at once. The grid scrolls by itself when you reach the edge.</li>
<li>Select a whole day, or everything, in one tap.</li>
<li>Move items to another folder or to a new one (with folder search), share them, or send them to the trash.</li>
</ul>

<b>Edit</b>
<ul>
<li>Photos: crop (free or fixed ratio), rotate, mirror. Photo details (date, camera, location) are kept.</li>
<li>Videos: trim with a live preview, crop, rotate, mirror, and export short clips (up to 15 seconds) as animated GIFs.</li>
<li>GIFs: crop, rotate, mirror, change speed and looping. Animated GIFs play in the viewer.</li>
<li>Open and edit images from other apps (Edit with, Open with, Share).</li>
<li>Save as a copy or replace the original. Choose each time, or set a default, separately for photos and videos.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission: nothing ever leaves your phone.</li>
<li>No ads, no trackers, no analytics.</li>
<li>Optional "media management" permission (Android 12+) to edit, move and delete without a confirmation every time.</li>
</ul>

Material You design with light, dark and pure-black themes. Available in English and French.
